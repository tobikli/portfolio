<p>
	A project I built during the <strong>itestra Summer Camp 2024</strong>.
</p>

<p>
	It is an AR HoloLens game inspired by Piano Tiles. You can track a virtual keyboard onto your
	real keyboard and play a handful of piano sheets. Notes fall onto the real piano keys to
	indicate when each key should be played.
</p>

<p>
	You can also import your own sheets.
</p>
