<p>
	During the intro course of <strong>iPraktikum 2024/25</strong> at TUM, I developed a Swift app
	that helps users track, budget, and plan trips and vacations.
</p>

<p>
	Users can define a budget, track spending, and manage expenses for flights, hotels, food, and
	activities. The app visualizes budget usage with multiple statistics.
</p>

<p>
	It also features a city map for the trip destination and live weather data via API calls.
</p>
